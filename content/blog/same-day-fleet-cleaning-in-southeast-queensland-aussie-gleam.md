## A Few Practical Tips for Managing Fleet Presentation Year-Round

Same-day cleaning is a great solution when you need it — but building some routine into your fleet maintenance saves you the stress of scrambling at the last minute.

- **Set a regular cleaning schedule** based on how hard your vehicles work. High-use vehicles in dusty or coastal environments might need attention fortnightly; lighter-use vehicles might be fine monthly.
- **Brief your drivers** on keeping the interior tidy between cleans. Small habits make a big difference.
- **Consider a fleet contract** if you're running five or more vehicles — it locks in your pricing and takes the scheduling off your plate.
