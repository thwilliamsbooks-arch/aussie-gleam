End of financial year sneaks up fast — and if you run a fleet in Southeast Queensland, June 30 has a habit of arriving before you've ticked everything off the list. EOFY fleet cleaning might not be the first thing on your radar when you're chasing invoices and reconciling accounts, but it's one of those practical tasks that pays off in more ways than one. Whether you're managing five utes or fifty vans, getting your vehicles professionally cleaned before the financial year closes is worth making time for.

## Why EOFY Is the Right Time for a Fleet Clean

There's a reason savvy fleet managers schedule their financial year fleet clean around this time of year — and it's not just about appearances.

### Fleet Condition Affects Asset Value

At the end of every financial year, many businesses conduct asset reviews, prepare for audits, or organise fleet insurance renewals. A vehicle that's been properly maintained — including its exterior and interior condition — holds its value better and presents more accurately in any formal assessment. If you're upgrading vehicles or trading in older ones around EOFY, turning up with clean, well-presented vehicles can make a real difference to what you're offered.

### It's a Smart Time for a Thorough Inspection

A professional clean isn't just cosmetic. When a detailer is working over every surface of your vehicles, it naturally prompts a closer look at the overall condition — scratches, dents, sun damage, or interior wear that might have been overlooked during the busy year. Southeast Queensland's climate is hard on vehicles. Between the summer humidity, UV intensity, storm debris, and the dust that builds up on unsealed roads out toward the Scenic Rim and Ipswich fringes, your fleet takes a beating year-round. June is a good natural checkpoint.

### Documentation and Presentation for EOFY Reporting

If your vehicles are photographed or inspected as part of end-of-year processes, having them clean and presentable reflects well on how your business manages its assets. It's a small detail that adds up, especially for larger fleets where vehicle condition is a factor in insurance, leasing, or finance agreements.

## The Queensland Factor: What the Year Does to Your Fleet

It's worth acknowledging what Southeast Queensland actually puts your vehicles through.

Summer brings heavy rain, which sounds helpful but actually deposits grime, insects, and road contamination across paintwork. High UV levels between October and March accelerate oxidation and fade. Then there's the everyday accumulation — fuel depot grime, construction site dust, food and coffee spills inside the cab, and the general wear of being a working vehicle.

By the time June rolls around, most fleet vehicles in Brisbane, Logan, Gold Coast, and surrounding areas are carrying a full year's worth of that exposure. A thorough June fleet service doesn't just clean the surface — it helps protect the paint and interior from further deterioration going into the next year.

## What a Professional Fleet Clean Actually Covers

A proper EOFY vehicle maintenance clean goes well beyond a quick rinse.

Depending on the package, a professional detail typically includes:

- **Exterior hand wash and decontamination** — removing embedded dirt, insect residue, and road tar that a standard wash misses
- **Tyre and wheel cleaning** — often the most visibly neglected area on work vehicles
- **Interior vacuum and wipe-down** — dashboards, consoles, door trims, and upholstery
- **Window and glass cleaning** — inside and out, which directly affects driver visibility and safety
- **Condition treatments** — depending on the package, this might include tyre dressing, trim conditioning, or paint protection

For businesses looking at more intensive work, a full detail can include paint correction, upholstery shampooing, and odour treatment — particularly useful for vehicles that carry clients or represent your brand on the road.
