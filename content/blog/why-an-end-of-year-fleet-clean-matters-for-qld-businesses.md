If your fleet has been putting in the hard yards across Southeast Queensland all year, chances are it's showing it. Dust from Scenic Rim back roads, salt air from the Gold Coast, sun fade from relentless Queensland summers — by November and December, most commercial vehicles are well overdue for a proper end of year fleet clean. It's one of those things that's easy to push down the priority list when your team is busy, but getting it sorted before the Christmas break can make a real difference heading into the new year.

## Why the End of the Year Is the Right Time to Clean Your Fleet

There's a reason savvy fleet managers schedule a Christmas fleet clean before the holiday shutdown. It's not just about appearances — though turning up to client sites in clean, professional vehicles absolutely matters. It's about timing.

The end of the year gives you a natural reset point. Vehicles are often parked up over the Christmas and New Year break, which means there's no operational disruption if you schedule a clean while the team is off. You come back in January to a fleet that looks sharp, which sets a strong tone for the year ahead.

There's also a practical maintenance angle. A thorough clean at year's end means you or your mechanic can actually see the condition of the bodywork, check for rust, identify any dings or damage that's been hiding under a layer of grime, and deal with issues before they become expensive problems.

### Queensland Conditions Are Hard on Vehicles

It's worth remembering that Southeast Queensland is a genuinely tough environment for vehicles. The UV index here is extreme compared to most parts of the world, which accelerates paint oxidation and fade. Summer storms leave mineral deposits on paintwork. Coastal areas like the Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast add salt to the mix, which can work into seals and undercarriage if left unchecked.

If your vehicles spend time on unsealed roads in areas like the Scenic Rim or rural Logan, you're also dealing with dust, mud, and debris that can work into door seals, vent grilles, and under panels over time. A proper year end vehicle maintenance routine — including a thorough external and internal clean — helps protect your assets and extend the life of the paintwork and finish.

## What a Professional End of Year Fleet Clean Actually Covers

There's a difference between a quick rinse and a proper fleet reset. Here's what a professional mobile clean should include for commercial vehicles:

**Exterior wash and rinse** — removing road grime, bird droppings, insect residue, and surface contamination that eats into clear coat over time.

**Wheel and tyre clean** — brake dust is corrosive. Getting it off the wheels before Christmas means less damage over the break.

**Interior vacuum and wipe-down** — your drivers spend a lot of time in these vehicles. A clean cab is better for morale and professionalism.

**Window clean inside and out** — visibility and presentation in one.

**Spot treatment for stubborn contamination** — tar, tree sap, and heavy insect residue often need specific attention.
