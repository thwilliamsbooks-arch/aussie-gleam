### What's Included in a Scheduled Fleet Wash?

A regular vehicle cleaning visit typically covers:

- **Exterior hand wash** — removing road grime, bird droppings, insects, and general buildup
- **Wheel and tyre clean** — tyres cop a hiding in Queensland conditions
- **Window and mirror clean** — important for both presentation and driver safety
- **Interior vacuum and wipe-down** — because your drivers spend a lot of time in these vehicles

The specifics can be tailored to your fleet's needs. Some clients want a quick, consistent exterior wash on a fortnightly basis, while others prefer a more detailed clean rotated across their vehicles over time. We're flexible — because no two fleets are the same.

## Why Regular Vehicle Cleaning Is More Than Just Cosmetic

It's easy to think of fleet washing as a cosmetic exercise — something you do when the boss is coming to visit, or before a client presentation. But consistent, scheduled cleaning actually has real operational and financial benefits.

### It Protects Your Paint and Panels

Queensland's UV is brutal. When grime, bird droppings, and insect residue sit on a vehicle's paintwork for weeks at a time, they can cause etching and corrosion that's expensive to repair. Regular washing removes these contaminants before they do lasting damage, which directly extends the life of your fleet.

### It Keeps Your Brand Working For You

Your fleet is a moving billboard. Every time a dirty, faded vehicle with peeling stickers pulls up at a client's site or parks on a main road, it sends a message about your business — and not the one you want to send. A clean, well-presented fleet says you take pride in your work. That matters, especially in competitive industries like trades, logistics, and civil construction.

### It's Better for Drivers Too

People perform better when they're working in a clean environment. A tidy vehicle — inside and out — sets a professional standard and shows your team that you invest in their workspace. It's a small thing that contributes to morale more than most people realise.

## How to Get the Frequency Right for Your Fleet

Not every fleet needs the same schedule. A bi-weekly fleet cleaning routine works well for most commercial operations, but the right cadence depends on a few things:

- **How often your vehicles are on the road** — higher usage means more buildup
- **What environments they're operating in** — coastal, construction, or agricultural settings are harder on vehicles than urban commutes
- **Your industry's presentation standards** — customer-facing businesses generally need more frequent cleaning than back-of-house logistics
